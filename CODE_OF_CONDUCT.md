# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, constructive space for users and contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient when discussing bugs, design decisions, and hardware limitations.
- Provide actionable technical feedback and credit others' work.
- Respect privacy, licensing, and the safety of other people's devices.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or observe a conduct problem, contact the repository maintainer through an appropriate private channel where possible. Do not publish sensitive personal details in an issue. The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation when needed.

This policy applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project-managed spaces.
